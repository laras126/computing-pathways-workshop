We invite position papers describing ongoing or completed work related to the design, deployment, or study of systems that support collective action.

Submissions may address, but are not limited to:

- Participatory design with organizers and communities
- Civic and political technology
- Labor and workplace tools
- Crisis coordination and mutual aid infrastructure
- Maintenance, repair, and community stewardship of technology
- The ethics and politics of HCI research in movement contexts
- Historical and comparative perspectives on organizing tools

Papers should be **{{ workshop.cfp.format }}** and submitted as a PDF. We are especially interested in work that reflects on its own situatedness — who built this, for whom, and under what conditions.

At least one author must register for the workshop and for one full day of the conference.
